Ada keeps the last working relay mast above a harbour that has not seen a
sunrise in nine years. Every dawn she climbs the mast and sends the same test
pulse into the fog, and every dawn the fog swallows it.

This morning the pulse comes back.

She reads the returning signal twice before she believes it: another mast is
answering, somewhere past the water. Ada carries the reply down the harbour
stair, because a signal that is not shared is only a rumour.
