The loft ledger is the only record of who repairs the public clocks of Vell. Ren
has copied entries into it since apprenticeship: tower, fault, date, and the name
of the hand that fixed it.

This week three towers carry repairs with no name beside them.

Ren walks out to tower seven and finds fresh solder, a bent minute hand set true,
and a scaffold plank left exactly where a short person would need it. The work is
unmistakable. It belongs to Sable, who has been repairing the city's clocks unpaid
for years and logging none of it.

Ren goes back to the loft and writes the missing name into the ledger, seven times.
