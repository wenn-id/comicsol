# Editorial Signal

Comic Sol's thumbnail treats the product as both a working tool and a finished comic artifact. A cinematic slice of generated manga carries the visual proof; restrained typography identifies the engine without competing with the story. The composition should feel like an editorial cover for a serious creative instrument, not a generic AI gradient.

The palette is built from Comic Sol's existing visual language: charcoal ink, warm paper and brass, amber light, and a controlled cyan technical glow. Warm color establishes craft and narrative; cyan appears as a precise signal for automation and computation. Dark tonal mass creates a stage for the title and keeps the image legible at Devpost thumbnail size.

Typography is sparse and structural. Comic Neue Bold gives the product name a hand-lettered comic connection, while Noto Sans keeps technical labels crisp. Information is arranged as a small hierarchy: product name first, capability statement second, pipeline stages last. Fine rules, corner marks, and measured spacing suggest deterministic tooling without turning the thumbnail into a dashboard.

The artwork remains the dominant proof point. The crop preserves the explorer, amber machine light, and cyan device contrast, while a translucent editorial panel protects the title from the original page lettering. Every element stays inside generous square margins so the image remains clear in feeds, project cards, and mobile previews.

Execution target: meticulous, quiet, premium. No decorative clutter, no fabricated UI, no claims beyond the product's real workflow. The result should read in one second as: Comic Sol makes complete comics from a prompt.

## Canvas contract

- Output: 1600 × 1600 PNG, RGB/RGBA-safe
- Canonical thumbnail: `assets/comic-sol-thumbnail.png`, composed from real Comic Sol sample artwork
- Title: `COMIC SOL`
- Descriptor: `AI-DRIVEN COMIC PRODUCTION`
- Pipeline cue: `PLAN · GENERATE · QA · LETTER · EXPORT`
- Primary colors: charcoal, cream, brass/amber, cyan
- Text-safe margin: 120 px minimum
- No external logo or unverified product claim
